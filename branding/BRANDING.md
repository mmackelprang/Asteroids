# Asteroids — brand guide

**Proposed display name:** Asteroids
**Tagline:** *Vectors never die.*

## Why this name

The game's name, kept. The white-wireframe treatment is the game's whole aesthetic.

## The mark

The triangular ship under thrust beside a craggy rock — pure vector, as the XY monitor drew it.

## Palette

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Space | `#0A0A0F` | Background |
| Vector White | `#FFFFFF` | Ship, rock |
| Thrust Gold | `#FFD166` | Flame |

> **Trademark note:** Asteroids is an Atari title; present the project as a fan recreation.
